# Office core: draft term list (for review)

100 terms every office worker should know, whatever the role. 10 groups of 10.
This is the review draft. Once it is agreed it becomes `content/cards/office-core.json`
(see `content/cards/AUTHORING.md`: cards, tiers 1 to 5, hand-written scenario questions).

Plain-English meanings are one line each so the card writer has a fixed target.

## 1. Communication
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| email | a written message sent electronically to one or more people |
| cc and bcc | cc shows other recipients you copied in; bcc copies them without the others seeing |
| reply all | answering everyone on a message, not just the sender |
| attachment | a file sent along with a message |
| subject line | the short title that tells the reader what a message is about |
| out-of-office reply | an automatic answer telling senders you are away |
| memo | a short formal note sent inside an organisation |
| announcement | a message that tells many people about a change or event |
| feedback | what you tell someone about how their work or behaviour landed |
| follow-up | a later message or action that checks something was done |

## 2. Meetings
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| agenda | the list of topics a meeting will cover |
| minutes | the written record of what was discussed and decided |
| action item | a task given to a named person at a meeting |
| attendee | a person who is invited to or present at a meeting |
| calendar invite | a message that puts a meeting in people's diaries |
| stand-up | a very short daily team check-in |
| one-to-one | a private regular meeting between a person and their manager |
| briefing | a short session that gives people the facts they need |
| quorum | the minimum number of people needed for a meeting's decisions to count |
| any other business | the end of an agenda where extra topics can be raised |

## 3. Projects and planning
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| project | a piece of work with a goal, an end date and a team |
| task | one small piece of work someone is asked to do |
| milestone | a marker for an important point on the way to the finish |
| deadline | the date or time something must be finished by |
| deliverable | a thing a project promises to hand over |
| scope | what a piece of work includes, and what it does not |
| dependency | something that must happen before another thing can start |
| priority | how urgent or important something is compared with other work |
| timeline | the plan of what happens when |
| kickoff | the first meeting that starts a project |

## 4. Reporting and information
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| report | a structured document that tells readers what happened or what was found |
| summary | a short version that keeps only the main points |
| dashboard | one screen that shows key numbers at a glance |
| metric | a number used to measure something |
| KPI | a key performance indicator: a metric that shows whether goals are being met |
| target | the number or result you are aiming for |
| forecast | an estimate of what will happen later |
| draft | an early version that is not yet final |
| final version | the agreed copy that replaces all drafts |
| source | where a fact or figure came from |

## 5. People and teams
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| onboarding | helping a new person settle in and start work |
| induction | the first formal introduction to the organisation and its rules |
| probation | an early trial period for a new employee |
| line manager | the person you report to day to day |
| team lead | the person who guides a team's daily work |
| colleague | someone you work with |
| job description | a written list of what a role does |
| appraisal | a formal review of how someone is doing in their job |
| handover | passing work and knowledge to the next person |
| notice period | how long someone must keep working after resigning or being let go |

## 6. Working patterns
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| shift | a set block of working hours |
| rota | a schedule that says who works when |
| annual leave | paid time off for holidays |
| sick leave | time off, normally paid, because of illness |
| flexitime | choosing your start and finish times within agreed limits |
| hybrid working | splitting time between an office and home |
| remote working | working away from the office, usually from home |
| overtime | work done beyond normal hours |
| timesheet | a record of hours worked |
| absence | time away from work, planned or not |

## 7. Money and admin
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| invoice | a bill a supplier sends asking to be paid |
| purchase order | a document that approves a purchase before it is made |
| budget | the amount of money set aside for something |
| expense | money you spend on work and claim back |
| receipt | proof that a payment was made |
| quote | a price offered before the work is agreed |
| payroll | the process of paying employees |
| cost | what something takes to buy or do |
| revenue | the money a business earns from selling |
| approval | official permission to go ahead |

## 8. Customers, products and services
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| customer | someone who buys from you |
| client | a customer who receives an ongoing professional service |
| product | something a business makes and sells |
| service | work done for someone, rather than a thing handed over |
| supplier | a business that provides goods or services to you |
| vendor | another word for a seller, often of software or equipment |
| contract | a written agreement that both sides are bound by |
| proposal | a document that offers a solution and a price |
| complaint | a statement that something was not good enough |
| support ticket | a logged request for help that can be tracked |

## 9. Tools and files
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| spreadsheet | a grid of rows and columns for numbers and lists |
| template | a ready-made document you copy and fill in |
| shared drive | online storage a whole team can open |
| permissions | the rules about who may see or change something |
| backup | a spare copy kept in case the original is lost |
| version history | the saved list of past changes to a file |
| notification | an alert that something needs your attention |
| user account | your personal identity and access on a system |
| software licence | permission to use software, often paid per person |
| helpdesk | the team you ask when a tool or device is not working |

## 10. Rules, risk and quality
| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| policy | a rule the organisation has decided everyone follows |
| procedure | the steps for carrying out a task the right way |
| compliance | following laws, rules and standards |
| confidential | not to be shared beyond those who need it |
| data protection | looking after personal information lawfully |
| risk | something that might go wrong, and how bad it would be |
| audit | an independent check that rules are being followed |
| incident | something unplanned that harms or could harm work |
| escalation | passing a problem up to someone with more authority |
| health and safety | keeping people from harm at work |

---

## Open points for review
- **UK or neutral wording?** Some terms are UK (annual leave, rota, flexitime; VAT is left out on purpose). Say if you want US variants (PTO, schedule) as accepted alternatives.
- **Overlap:** `communications-email` already covers email etiquette in depth. Here "email", "cc and bcc" and "reply all" stay vocabulary-level only.
- **Tiers:** about 60 terms would be tier 1 to 2 (everyone), 30 tier 3, 10 tier 4 to 5 (quorum, audit, escalation, dependency).
- **Left for the role lists:** finance depth (accrual, reconciliation), security (MFA, least privilege) and engineering (API, repo) terms are not here.
